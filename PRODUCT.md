# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BillFlow is used by Thai back-office admins and operations staff who review marketplace, email, LINE, and import-driven bills before sending them into SML ERP. They work under repetitive daily volume, need fast scanning, and must avoid sending incorrect document data.

## Product Purpose

BillFlow reduces manual bill entry from 100+ daily records to near zero by extracting order data, mapping items, surfacing review issues, and routing approved bills into SML with traceable logs and recovery paths.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, supportive. The product should feel like a reliable review desk: quiet enough for repeated operational work, explicit when something blocks a user, and confident without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not make admin screens feel like marketing pages, dashboards full of decorative cards, or UI that relies on color alone. Avoid hiding critical prerequisites, using vague success/error messages, or making users infer which SML field will be affected.

## Design Principles

1. Show the operational state before asking users to act.
2. Make risky actions require visible prerequisites and clear disabled reasons.
3. Keep dense admin workflows compact, readable, and consistent across light and dark mode.
4. Preserve source traceability from marketplace order to BillFlow bill to SML document.
5. Prefer calm clarity over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, keyboard-operable dialogs and buttons, visible focus states, and labels that do not rely on color alone. Thai copy should be concise, specific, and fit compact desktop and mobile layouts.
